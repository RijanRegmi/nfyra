Features

Privacy-first from the ground up

Every design decision was made to keep your data off the internet and under your control.

📡
No Receiver App Required

Nfyra emulates a standard NFC Forum Type 4 Tag. Any Android or iPhone can receive your message — no app install needed on the other side.

🔒
Hardware-Backed Signatures

Every message is signed using an EC key stored in Android Keystore (StrongBox/TEE). Recipients with Nfyra verify it cryptographically in milliseconds.

⏱️
60-Second Expiry

Messages carry a timestamp and auto-expire after 60 seconds. Replay attacks are mathematically impossible thanks to per-message nonces.

🚫
Zero Internet Permission

INTERNET is strictly omitted from the manifest and validated in CI. The app literally cannot transmit data over Wi-Fi or cellular.

🔐
Encrypted Local History

Received messages are stored in AES-encrypted local storage via Android Keystore. Nothing leaves your device — ever.

🔗
Safe URL Handling

Only https:// URLs are accepted. Dangerous protocols are stripped. URLs never open automatically — always requiring your explicit confirmation.

How it works

Simple enough to explain in 4 steps

1

Type your message or paste a link

Open Nfyra on your Android device. Type any text or paste an https:// URL you want to share with someone nearby.

2

Tap "Share via NFC" and authenticate

A 30-second countdown begins. Your phone's HCE service arms itself, signing the payload with your hardware-backed key. Biometric authentication is enforced.

3

Hold both phones back-to-back

The NFC RF touch happens in under a second. No pairing, no Bluetooth, no Wi-Fi. Just physics — radio waves between two devices in close proximity.

4

Receiver sees the message instantly

With Nfyra: signature is verified, green badge appears, logged to encrypted history. Without the app: Android Tag Viewer or iPhone Safari shows it automatically.
