Child Safety Standards

App: Nfyra for Android  |  Last updated: October 13, 2026

Our position: Nfyra has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We prohibit it, and we will cooperate fully with law enforcement and child protection authorities.

1. Our commitment

Nfyra is committed to protecting children from sexual abuse and exploitation. Any use of Nfyra to create, share, request, store, or promote CSAE or CSAM is strictly forbidden, in any form and in any context.

2. What is prohibited

  • Sharing, sending, or linking to child sexual abuse material.
  • Sexualizing minors, including text-based sexual content involving minors.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to contact minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any other form of child exploitation.
  • Using the app to advertise or direct others to such content or activity.

3. How Nfyra works, and what that means for safety

Nfyra is an offline, peer-to-peer NFC app. Messages travel directly between two phones held together. There are no accounts, no servers, no public feeds, no chat rooms, and no search or discovery features. Strangers cannot find or message each other through the app, and the developer does not host or store user content.

Because of this design, the app has no way for an adult to find or contact children through Nfyra. We still hold ourselves to the standards on this page and will act on any concern that reaches us.

4. Reporting a concern

If you encounter or suspect CSAE related to Nfyra, contact us using the details below. Please include as much detail as you safely can. Do not forward or share the material itself; describe it instead.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

5. How we respond

  • We review every report promptly.
  • We remove or disable any feature or content that is confirmed to enable CSAE.
  • Where we become aware of CSAM, we will report it to the relevant national or regional authority, such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the United States, or the equivalent body in the relevant country.
  • We will preserve relevant information, where we hold any, and cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement.

6. Legal compliance

Nfyra complies with applicable child safety laws and Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, and we update these standards when laws or policies change.
