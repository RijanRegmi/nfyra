4. NFC (Near Field Communication) Usage

Nfyra's core functionality relies on NFC hardware. Here is exactly how NFC is used:

  • Sending: When you compose a message and tap "Share via NFC", the app activates Android's Host Card Emulation (HCE) service for up to 30 seconds. Your message is encoded as an NFC NDEF record and broadcast over short-range radio (<4 cm) to a nearby device.
  • Receiving: When your device is tapped by a sender, the NFC payload is read from the air interface and validated cryptographically before being shown to you.
  • No NFC data is stored remotely. NFC communication happens exclusively between two physical devices in close proximity. Nfyra does not relay, log, or upload NFC payloads anywhere.
  • Message expiry: All NFC payloads carry a 60-second expiration timestamp. Expired payloads are automatically rejected and never displayed.
✓ Short-range only (<4 cm) ✓ Payload expires in 60 s ✓ No relay server ✗ Never uploaded

5. Biometric Authentication

Nfyra uses Android's BiometricPrompt API to optionally gate the "arm HCE" action (enabling NFC sharing). This means:

  • The authentication challenge is presented and verified entirely by the Android OS and the device's hardware security module (TEE/StrongBox).
  • Nfyra never receives, processes, or stores any fingerprint template, face map, or other biometric raw data.
  • The app only receives a boolean success/failure result from the Android Biometric API.

You may disable the biometric gate in the app's Settings screen. Your biometric data is always governed by your device manufacturer's and Google's own privacy policies.

🔒

6. Local Data Storage

Nfyra stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Message History: Received and sent NFC messages are saved to an encrypted local database protected by an Android Keystore-backed AES key. This data never leaves your device.
  • Nonce Cache: A small encrypted cache of seen message nonces is maintained to prevent replay attacks. It stores only random identifiers, not message content.
  • Signing Keypair: A hardware-backed Ed25519 / ECDSA P-256 keypair is generated on first launch and stored in Android Keystore. The private key is non-exportable.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as biometric gate toggle are stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences.

You can permanently delete all locally stored data at any time via Settings → Clear All Data. The app also supports an auto-delete timer for message history.

8. Security Measures

Nfyra implements multiple layers of defense to protect your data and prevent abuse:

  • Hardware-backed cryptography: Signing keys are generated in Android Keystore with StrongBox / TEE backing where available. Private keys are non-exportable.
  • Ed25519 / ECDSA P-256 signatures: Every NFC payload is cryptographically signed. Receivers verify the signature before accepting any message.
  • Anti-replay protection: Each payload carries a unique nonce. Previously seen nonces are rejected from a persistent encrypted cache.
  • Expiry enforcement: Payloads older than 60 seconds are automatically rejected, regardless of signature validity.
  • URL sanitization: Only https:// URLs are accepted. Dangerous schemes (javascript:, file:, data:, intent:) and control characters are stripped. URLs are never auto-opened; an explicit confirmation dialog is always shown.
  • Memory wiping: Armed HCE payloads are zeroed from memory immediately after a successful NFC read or on timeout.
  • FLAG_SECURE: Message screens block OS-level screenshots and screen recordings.
  • No backups: android:allowBackup="false" prevents data from being copied to cloud or ADB backups.

11. Your Rights & Data Control

Because Nfyra stores no data remotely and operates with no accounts, exercising most privacy rights is as simple as using your device:

  • Access: All data is on your device. You can view your message history directly in the app.
  • Deletion: Go to Settings → Clear All Data to permanently erase all locally stored messages, keys, and preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes all data.
  • Portability: You can screenshot or copy any message from the history screen at any time.
  • Correction: Message content is set at send time by the sender. Received messages can be deleted from your local history.

Since no data is transmitted to us, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf from a remote server.
